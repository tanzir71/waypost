# Skill labels

This small original Waypost seed vocabulary is dedicated to the public domain
under CC0-1.0. It contains common technology and skill names, not an ESCO,
O*NET, or proprietary Lightcast dataset. Source-attributed taxonomy subsets
are kept separately with their own licenses.

## O*NET® subset

onet.json contains selected, verbatim Software Skills workplace-example labels
from the [O*NET® 31.0 Database](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html), by the
U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA).
Used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Waypost selects unique labels and omits occupational associations, frequencies,
and weights. No other changes are made to the labels. The subset and its changes
are inspectable in onet.json. USDOL/ETA has not endorsed or tested Waypost.
O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. No Lightcast data is bundled.
