Waypost

Privacy

Waypost processes job descriptions and resumes in your browser. PDF, DOCX, text and JSON Resume uploads are parsed on this device with local libraries. Uploading a resume does not send it to a server. The landing demo keeps its upload in memory; JSON exports happen only when you choose them.

Local storage

App workspace records are stored in IndexedDB. Original resume uploads are stored in OPFS where supported; if that storage is unavailable, the app tells you and saves extracted text. Appearance preferences are stored locally. Legacy ats-scoring data is imported once and its original localStorage backup is kept. Demo samples are fetched from this website and seeded once. Clear sample data keeps personal records. Clearing all site storage removes local preferences, workspace records and original files.

Network requests

Waypost's demo and PWA modes load assets only from the website's own origin. There are no third-party fonts, analytics or tracking cookies. Your host may log requests for page and asset URLs. External source links open the sites you choose to visit.

Offline and shared inputs

The service worker caches this app’s public assets and documentation. Parser files are cached after first use. Private input links use fragments, which are not sent to the host. The operating-system share protocol uses GET parameters; an installed, controlling worker handles them locally without caching or forwarding the values, and the page removes the query. A shared GET URL opened without that worker may reach the host. Installation preferences and visit counts stay in local browser storage.

Optional connections

Encrypted sync is optional and off by default. Your own same-origin HTTPS server stores encrypted workspace records; passphrases and recovery codes stay in the browser. Calendar publishing is a separate opt-in: selected titles and dates are stored as plaintext and are readable by anyone with the revocable subscription URL. Connected devices update an enabled feed after syncing. Disconnecting sync leaves a published feed online; revoke it in Deadlines to disable its URL. Calendar apps may retain copies they already downloaded. Replacing vault keys or deleting the server vault revokes its feed. Optional AI is still being built and stays off; bring-your-own-key AI will be clearly labelled as sending selected text off-device, with accept/reject suggestions.

Your exports

Workspace JSON exports and folder backups contain your records and extracted text, excluding credentials and internal keys. They do not include original OPFS uploads. Imports are reviewed before saving, keep newer local records, and leave external connections off. Match reports, parsed text and JSON Resume exports contain the text you choose to include. Keep your exports somewhere you trust. No account, email reset, or third-party sign-in is required.
