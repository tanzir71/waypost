Waypost

Privacy

The live demo processes job descriptions and resumes in your browser. Uploading a text resume here does not send it to a server. Copy JSON and Download JSON export only when you choose them.

Local storage

Appearance preferences are stored on this device. Demo samples are fetched from this website. The app foundation includes a sample-data view; persistent workspace storage is coming with the full tools. Clearing site storage removes local preferences and data.

Network requests

Waypost's demo and PWA modes load assets only from the website's own origin. There are no third-party fonts, analytics or tracking cookies. Your host may log requests for page and asset URLs. External source links open the sites you choose to visit.

Optional connections

Encrypted sync and AI are off by default and have not shipped yet. Future sync stores encrypted workspace records on your own server. An opt-in published calendar will expose selected titles and dates through a revocable link. Bring-your-own-key AI will be clearly labelled as sending selected text off-device, with suggestions you can accept or reject.

Your exports

Workspace and match exports contain the text you choose to include. Keep your exports somewhere you trust. No account, email reset, or third-party sign-in is required.
